# Positioning and Messaging

Date: September 7, 2026

Scope: Planning only. Messaging below is draft language and must be approved by the owner before public use.

## Recommended Positioning

Softzino is a Bangladesh and Singapore software engineering partner for B2B companies that need custom software, MVPs, web applications, QA, cloud support, and flexible engineering teams.

Short version: Build custom software and extend your engineering team with Softzino.

Do not say Softzino is USA-based, Canada-based, UK-based, Europe-based, or Australia-based unless the owner verifies the local office, sales coverage, and legal status.
