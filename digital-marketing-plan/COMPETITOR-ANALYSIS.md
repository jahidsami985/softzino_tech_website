# Competitor Analysis

Date: September 7, 2026

Scope: Planning only. Competitor notes combine observed public positioning with strategic inference. Do not quote, compare, or name competitors in public campaigns without a fresh legal and brand review.

## How To Read This

Softzino has two competitor groups:

1. Direct service competitors: software development, staff augmentation, and product engineering companies.
2. Search competitors: directories and marketplace pages that rank for buyer-intent keywords.

The most important lesson is not that Softzino should copy them. It is that buyers expect proof: case studies, reviews, delivery model clarity, technology coverage, pricing guidance, and easy contact paths.
