# Softzino Digital Marketing Plan - Executive Summary

Date: September 7, 2026

Scope: Planning only. This document does not approve website edits, SEO implementation, paid campaigns, CRM changes, Git commits, deployments, or public claims.

## Business Owner Summary

Softzino has a strong B2B software services base, but the current marketing system is not yet ready to scale aggressively. The biggest opportunity is to focus the market message around a small number of high-intent services, prove those services with real project evidence, and make every lead source trackable before spending heavily.

The recommended first growth motion is: custom software development plus dedicated development teams for English-speaking international buyers, positioned honestly as a Bangladesh and Singapore engineering partner. Softzino should not claim to be USA-based, Canada-based, Europe-based, or local to a market unless that office, team, and service coverage are verified by the owner.

## Recommended First ICP

Primary ICP: CTOs, founders, product leaders, and operations leaders at funded startups, B2B SaaS companies, and growth-stage SMEs that need a custom product, MVP, web application, or dedicated engineering team.

Primary pages: https://softzino.com/solutions/custom-software-solutions/, https://softzino.com/hire-developers/, https://softzino.com/services/dedicated-development-team/.

## Recommended First Market

First market: United States B2B software buyers searching in English, served remotely from Softzino's verified Bangladesh and Singapore presence.

Reason: the current audit includes one keyword data point showing USA search demand for "custom software development company", and the website history mentions early US client work. Do not position Softzino as a USA company unless the owner verifies an active USA office, legal entity, sales team, and client-facing availability.

Relevant files: `seo-audit/KEYWORD-CLUSTERS.csv`, `app/about/page.tsx`, `components/layout/Footer.tsx`.
