# 90-Day Roadmap

Date: September 7, 2026

Scope: Planning only. This roadmap should be approved by the owner before implementation begins.

## Goal

In 90 days, Softzino should have a trustworthy marketing foundation: verified claims, working lead capture, basic attribution, fixed technical SEO foundations, strengthened Tier 1 pages, and the first small campaign tests.

## Roadmap

| Week | Focus | Work | Output | Relevant URL or file |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Verification | Confirm offices, services, claims, markets, clients, products, and sales owner | Approved claim sheet | `digital-marketing-plan/BUSINESS-INFORMATION-REQUIRED.md` |
| 2 | Measurement | Define GA4, GTM, GSC, CRM fields, conversion events, UTM rules | Measurement plan approved | `digital-marketing-plan/MEASUREMENT-AND-ATTRIBUTION.md` |
| 3 | Lead routing | Test Hire Developers form and contact paths | Lead delivery verified | `components/TalentRequestForm.tsx` |
| 4 | Technical SEO | Prepare sitemap, robots, canonical, metadata, schema, analytics requirements | Implementation brief | `seo-audit/TECHNICAL-SEO-AUDIT.md` |
| 5 | Keyword data | Collect missing search volume, CPC, keyword difficulty, current rankings, competitor pages | Updated keyword sheet | `seo-audit/KEYWORD-CLUSTERS.csv` |
| 6 | Custom software page | Improve proof, messaging, CTA, FAQs, internal links | Optimized page brief | https://softzino.com/solutions/custom-software-solutions/ |
| 7 | Hire developers journey | Improve lead form, roles, engagement model clarity, response expectations | Conversion brief | https://softzino.com/hire-developers/ |
| 8 | Dedicated team and staff augmentation | Differentiate team-model pages and prevent keyword overlap | Page briefs | https://softzino.com/services/dedicated-development-team/ |
| 9 | MVP and web application pages | Add packages, examples, FAQs, internal links | Page briefs | https://softzino.com/services/mvp-development/ |
| 10 | Proof assets | Draft 2 case studies and 1 product proof story | Case-study drafts | https://softzino.com/off-the-shelf-solutions/ |
| 11 | LinkedIn and email prep | Build content calendar, founder posts, nurture emails, partner list | Campaign assets | `digital-marketing-plan/CONTENT-CALENDAR.csv` |
| 12 | Soft launch campaigns | Start organic LinkedIn and limited retargeting if tracking works | First campaign test | `digital-marketing-plan/CAMPAIGN-PLAN.csv` |
| 13 | Review and decide | Review KPIs, lead quality, sales feedback, next budget | 90-day report and next-quarter plan | `digital-marketing-plan/KPI-SCORECARD.csv` |

## First 30 Days Should Not Include

| Avoid | Reason | Relevant file |
|---|---|---|
| Large paid search spend | Tracking and lead routing must be proven first | `digital-marketing-plan/BUDGET-SCENARIOS.md` |
| New country pages | Office and market claims need verification | `seo-audit/SEO-DATA-REQUIRED.md` |
| Unapproved client stories | Permission and outcomes need confirmation | `digital-marketing-plan/BUSINESS-INFORMATION-REQUIRED.md` |
| Publishing broad blog content | Revenue pages and proof are more urgent | `digital-marketing-plan/CONTENT-STRATEGY.md` |
