# ICP and Buyer Personas

Date: September 7, 2026

Scope: Planning only. Personas are working hypotheses until sales history, CRM data, win/loss notes, and owner input are verified.

## Recommended Primary ICP

Primary ICP: English-speaking B2B companies that need custom software delivery or engineering capacity but do not want to hire a full in-house team immediately.

Best-fit buyers:
- CTOs and technical founders at funded startups.
- Product leaders at B2B SaaS companies.
- Operations leaders at SMEs with manual workflows.
- Agency or consultancy leaders needing delivery capacity.

Primary pages: https://softzino.com/solutions/custom-software-solutions/, https://softzino.com/hire-developers/, https://softzino.com/services/dedicated-development-team/.
