# 12-Month Roadmap

Date: September 7, 2026

Scope: Planning only. This roadmap should be adjusted after the first 90-day KPI review.

## 12-Month Objective

Build a measurable B2B demand-generation system around Softzino's strongest services, then expand only after proof, tracking, and sales conversion data show what is working.

## Quarterly Roadmap

| Quarter | Main goal | Workstreams | Key outputs | Relevant URL or file |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Build foundation | Verification, lead routing, analytics, technical SEO, Tier 1 page briefs | Working measurement system and first optimized pages | `digital-marketing-plan/90-DAY-ROADMAP.md` |
| Q2 | Build proof and campaign motion | Publish case studies, improve directory profiles, launch narrow campaigns | Case-study library and first qualified-lead benchmarks | `digital-marketing-plan/CAMPAIGN-PLAN.csv` |
| Q3 | Expand content and partnerships | Add guides, comparison pages, partner campaigns, email nurture | Stronger organic funnel and partner pipeline | `digital-marketing-plan/CONTENT-STRATEGY.md` |
| Q4 | Scale proven channels | Expand paid search, retargeting, review platforms, regional pages if verified | Budget scaling decision and next-year plan | `digital-marketing-plan/BUDGET-SCENARIOS.md` |
