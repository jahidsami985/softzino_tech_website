## Campaign Governance

| Rule | Relevant file |
|---|---|
| Every campaign must use UTMs | `digital-marketing-plan/MEASUREMENT-AND-ATTRIBUTION.md` |
| Every campaign must route leads to a named owner | `digital-marketing-plan/BUSINESS-INFORMATION-REQUIRED.md` |
| Every campaign must have a page, offer, proof asset, and follow-up sequence | `digital-marketing-plan/CAMPAIGN-PLAN.csv` |
| Campaigns with paid spend require weekly review of cost per qualified lead | `digital-marketing-plan/KPI-SCORECARD.csv` |
