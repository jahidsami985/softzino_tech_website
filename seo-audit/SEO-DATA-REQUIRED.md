# SEO Data Required

Date: 2026-09-05

Purpose: list the business, analytics, keyword, competitor, and proof data required before moving from SEO planning to implementation.

## Keyword Data Needed

The CSV files intentionally do not invent search volume, CPC, ranking difficulty, or conversion potential numbers. Collect this data before final page creation or prioritization.

| Data Needed | Source | Segment Required | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly search volume | Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, Semrush, Moz, or equivalent | USA, Canada, UK, Europe, global English | Prioritize pages by demand |
| CPC | Google Keyword Planner or paid search tool | Same country segments | Estimate commercial value |
| Keyword difficulty | Ahrefs, Semrush, Moz, or equivalent | Same country segments | Estimate ranking effort |
| SERP features | Ahrefs/Semrush/manual review | Top 20 results per priority query | Understand if pages, directories, videos, maps, or guides dominate |
| Current rankings | Google Search Console and rank tracker | Last 3, 6, and 12 months | Identify existing traction and quick wins |
| Query impressions | Google Search Console Performance report | Query, page, country, device | Find terms already associated with Softzino |
| Organic CTR | Google Search Console Performance report | Query and page | Improve titles/meta for pages with impressions but low CTR |
| Current indexed pages | Google Search Console Indexing report | Production domain | Find crawl/index issues |
| Backlinks and referring domains | Ahrefs/Semrush/Majestic or equivalent | Domain and page level | Understand authority gap versus competitors |
| Competitor top pages | Ahrefs/Semrush/manual SERP | USA, UK, Canada, Europe | Identify page types and content depth needed |

## Business Owner Information Required

| Question | Required Answer | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| What are the top 3 revenue priorities for SEO? | Rank services by expected revenue and margin | Prevent chasing traffic that does not sell |
| Which countries are priority markets? | USA, UK, Canada, Europe, Singapore, Australia, Bangladesh, other | Determines keyword segmentation and page strategy |
| Which markets have real proof? | Office, clients, contracts, time-zone support, team members, compliance | Prevents thin or misleading location pages |
| What is the correct legal/company identity? | Legal name, brand name, registration, founding details | Needed for Organization schema and trust content |
| What are the correct office addresses? | One verified address set for Bangladesh, Singapore, USA, Australia | Footer, imprint, LocalBusiness schema, regional trust |
| Is "100+ professionals" current? | Current team size and date verified | Avoid outdated proof |
| Can listed clients be named publicly? | Approved names, quotes, logos, industries | Required for case studies and proof sections |
| Which project outcomes can be claimed? | Metrics, date ranges, attribution, client permission | Required before using numerical claims |
| What is the minimum qualified lead? | Budget, project size, team size, service need, geography | Shapes CTAs and qualification form |
| How fast can teams start? | Time to shortlist, interview, onboard, and scale | Important for hiring and staff augmentation pages |
| What are standard engagement models? | Fixed scope, T&M, staff augmentation, dedicated team, managed team, recruitment | Required to differentiate overlapping pages |
| How is IP handled? | Contract/IP ownership terms | Common buyer objection |
| What security/compliance can be claimed? | NDA, GDPR, SOC 2, ISO, HIPAA, PCI, access control | Required for enterprise and Europe content |
| Who receives web leads? | Sales owner, email, CRM, SLA, backup contact | Required before SEO growth |
| What tools should track leads? | GA4, GTM, HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho, Pipedrive, Calendly, email | Required for attribution |

| Requirement | Implementation Detail |
|---|---|

| Google Search Console | Verify domain property, submit sitemap, monitor indexing and performance |
| Tag Manager or equivalent | Manage CTA and form events without frequent code releases |
| Form backend logs | Store submitted form data, timestamps, source, page, referrer, and status |

| Dashboard | Weekly report by page, query, country, service cluster, and lead quality |

## Competitor Data To Collect

For each target country or priority service, collect the top 10 to 20 ranking pages and capture:

- URL.
- Page type: service page, directory, comparison guide, case study, local page, marketplace, article.
- Title and H1.
- Word count estimate.
- Number of reviews/testimonials.
- Case studies or portfolio proof.
- Pricing/rate information.
- CTA type.
- FAQ count.
- Structured data found.
- Backlinks/referring domains.
- Countries or cities targeted.
- Service model positioning.
- Unique trust claims.

## Country-Specific Validation Checklist

Before creating USA, UK, Canada, or Europe pages, confirm:

1. Search volume and keyword difficulty by market.
2. Current impressions/clicks by country in Google Search Console.
3. Sales priority and lead quality by country.
4. At least one proof asset relevant to the market.
5. Time-zone overlap details.
6. Contract and payment support for that market.
7. IP ownership language.
8. Security/compliance claims.
9. Office/legal presence, if claimed.
10. Local terminology, such as "bespoke software" for UK pages if data supports it.

## Recommended Data Collection Sequence

1. Export last 16 months of Google Search Console data by query, page, country, and device.
2. Export GA4 or analytics landing page and conversion data for organic sessions.
3. Export CRM leads by source, service interest, country, deal stage, and revenue.
4. Pull keyword volume, CPC, and difficulty for the seed list by USA, UK, Canada, Europe, and global English.
5. Pull competitor top pages and backlink data for the top 20 commercial keywords.
6. Interview sales leadership to rank services by revenue, margin, and lead quality.
7. Collect case study and testimonial permissions.
8. Confirm official company, office, legal, and compliance information.
9. Finalize page prioritization using SEO demand plus revenue potential.
10. Begin implementation in the order defined in `SEO-PRIORITY-ROADMAP.md`.
