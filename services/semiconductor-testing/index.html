What it is

Comprehensive post-silicon validation, DFT, and ATE engineering services designed to guarantee the functional and structural integrity of integrated circuits.

Why you need it

To mitigate the enormous costs of silicon re-spins, ensure mission-critical reliability, and accelerate the complex journey from prototype to mass production.

Business Outcomes

Faster silicon yield, reduced time-to-market for chipsets, guaranteed silicon reliability, and cost-effective validation.

Industries

Semiconductors, Electronics, Automotive Electronics, AI Hardware, Aerospace, Telecommunications.

Our Offerings

Post-silicon Validation

Rigorous functional testing of prototype silicon to identify design flaws and ensure adherence to architectural specifications before volume manufacturing.

Design for Testability (DFT)

Integration of test infrastructure directly into the chip design to enable high-coverage manufacturing tests.

ATE Engineering

Development of highly optimized test programs and hardware interfaces for Automated Test Equipment.

Reliability Testing

Accelerated life testing, burn-in, and environmental stress screening to guarantee long-term operational stability.
