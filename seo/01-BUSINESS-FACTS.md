## Buyer Problems

- Legacy systems, spreadsheets, and disconnected tools slow operations.
- Existing software does not match the buyer's workflow or growth plan.
- Internal engineering teams lack capacity for roadmap delivery.
- Product teams need web, mobile, API, cloud, data, AI, QA, or DevOps skills.
- Buyers need a credible delivery partner but have limited proof about Softzino's market experience.

## Decision Criteria

- Clear service scope and recommended engagement model.
- Relevant technical capabilities and stack fit.
- Evidence of similar delivery work, product screenshots, or approved case studies.
- Security, IP, NDA, privacy, and data-handling clarity.
- Real lead routing, response ownership, and sales follow-up.
- Transparent discovery process, communication cadence, and delivery governance.

## Do Not Implement Until Approved

- Country or city landing pages.
- LocalBusiness schema for USA, UK, Canada, Germany, or other European markets.
- Client-logo or testimonial sections.
- Numerical outcomes, rankings, awards, certifications, or "best/top" claims.
- Cost or pricing guide pages.
- Paid search, retargeting, or aggressive SEO promotion.
